## About us

ARIFEX Lab is an undergraduate research lab founded in 2025 by Md. Arifuzzaman, Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at Leading University. We build machine learning systems for healthcare, security and everyday life, and we care just as much about *why* a model makes a decision as about how accurate it is.

The lab gives undergraduate students hands-on research experience, from designing a study and collecting data to building models and publishing the results.

## What we work on

**Computer vision and medical image analysis**
Deep learning for disease detection from medical images, including kidney disease diagnosis from CT scans, multi-class chest disease prediction from X-rays with automated report generation, and osteoporosis classification. We also apply vision to agriculture, such as tea leaf and fish disease detection.

**Health data analytics**
Explainable models for diabetes, obesity and respiratory risk, built on hospital laboratory records and survey data collected by our team.

**Natural language processing**
Language technology for Bangla, an under-resourced language with over 250 million speakers, including hate speech classification and misinformation detection.

**Cybersecurity for connected systems**
Intrusion detection for connected vehicles that fuses V2X and CAN bus signals, obfuscated XSS detection, and the robustness of machine learning security detectors.

**Explainable AI and human behavior**
SHAP, LIME and partial dependence analysis to make models interpretable, applied to studies of sleep, stress, daily routines and productivity among university students.

## Highlights

- 🏆 **Funded project:** AI-based multi-class chest disease prediction and automated diagnostic reporting (RPCC & CRISP, Leading University, 2025–2026)
- 📄 **13 papers at IEEE conferences** since 2025, including *BanglaIEHS*, a new dataset and multi-task framework for target-aware Bengali hate speech classification (IEEE COMPAS 2026)
- 🏥 **Primary data collection** with physicians and students, including 1,000+ hospital laboratory records and surveys of 800+ university students

## Our goal

We want to build AI that people can **trust and use**: models that perform well on real data from our own region, explain their reasoning to the experts who rely on them, and are evaluated honestly. Along the way, we aim to train a new generation of researchers in Bangladesh who can take a question from idea to publication.
