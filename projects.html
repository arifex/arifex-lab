Projects

Our ongoing research, from clinical imaging to everyday student health.

  • Toward Empirically Validated Cross-Layer Intrusion Detection for Connected Vehicles: Fusing V2X and CAN Bus Anomaly Signals

    Dataset
    Public CAN bus datasets (HCRL Car-Hacking or ROAD) and V2X datasets (VeReMi or MisbehaviorX), linked into a new cross-domain attack dataset through time alignment or co-simulation
    Goal
    Detect gateway pivot attacks, where an attacker enters through the external V2X interface and then attacks the in-vehicle CAN bus, by fusing both layers with a learned meta-model. The work also proposes a pivot-detection-latency metric and tests whether consensus- and trust-based defenses from collaborative perception transfer to V2X misbehavior detection.
    Research assistants
    Imtiaz Asif, Afrin Jahan, Pracurjo Chowdhury
