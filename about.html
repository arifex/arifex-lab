About the lab

The people who guide ARIFEX Lab, and how to reach us.

Supervisor

Md. Arifuzzaman

Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Leading University, Sylhet

I am originally from Gangni, Meherpur, Bangladesh. I completed my SSC at Gangni High School and my HSC at New Government Degree College, Rajshahi.

In 2012, I co-founded Gangni Gonit Poribar, a mathematics club that helps students strengthen their understanding of mathematics and science. Teaching through this club sparked my lifelong passion for education and mentorship.

In 2015–16, I was awarded the fully funded Indian Council for Cultural Relations (ICCR) scholarship by the Government of India, and earned my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering from Gujarat Technological University, Ahmedabad, India.

After returning to Bangladesh, I joined the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Leading University, Sylhet, as a faculty member. I also serve as advisor to Orpheus, the largest music club at Leading University.

My research is in artificial intelligence and machine learning, particularly deep learning for healthcare: medical imaging, biomedical signal processing and health data analytics. Under my supervision, the lab explores AI methods for disease diagnosis, predictive modeling and explainable decision support, including medical image analysis, disease localization and the development of clinical datasets.

Advisor

SK

Dr. Shafkat Kibria

Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Leading University, Sylhet

Dr. Shafkat Kibria was born in Howapara, Sylhet, Bangladesh, in 1978. He received his B.Sc. in Computer Science from East West University, Dhaka, in 2001 and his M.Sc. in Computer Science from Umeå University, Sweden, in 2005. He completed his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Sylhet, awarded on 23 March 2023. He joined the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Leading University in March 2020.
