About the lab

The people who guide ARIFEX Lab, and how to reach us.

Supervisor

Md. Arifuzzaman

Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Leading University, Sylhet, Bangladesh
Founder & Supervisor, ARIFEX Research Lab

I work on computer vision and deep learning, particularly medical image analysis, multimodal learning and explainable AI for clinical decision support. I also work on health data analytics, natural language processing for Bangla, and machine learning for cybersecurity, including intrusion detection for connected vehicles.

I am originally from Gangni, Meherpur, where in 2012 I co-founded Gangni Gonit Poribar, a mathematics club for school students. Teaching there sparked my lifelong interest in education and mentorship. I studied Computer Engineering in India as a fully funded ICCR scholar, and have taught at Leading University since 2021.

Mentor

SK

Dr. Shafkat Kibria

Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Leading University, Sylhet

Dr. Shafkat Kibria was born in Howapara, Sylhet, Bangladesh, in 1978. He received his B.Sc. in Computer Science from East West University, Dhaka, in 2001 and his M.Sc. in Computer Science from Umeå University, Sweden, in 2005. He completed his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Sylhet, awarded on 23 March 2023. He joined the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Leading University in March 2020.
