Welcome

ARIFEX Research Lab is a multidisciplinary group working at the frontiers of artificial intelligence in healthcare, lifestyle and human behavior. We combine machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and data science to build practical solutions for real-world problems.

Our work covers medical AI, including disease detection, predictive modeling and intelligent diagnostic systems, as well as wider applications such as analyzing lifestyle, sleep and productivity patterns. We aim to turn data into insights that improve lives.

Explore our publications, learn about our research projects, meet our team, or get in touch.
