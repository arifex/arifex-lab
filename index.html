Welcome

ARIFEX Research Lab is a multidisciplinary group working on artificial intelligence for healthcare, security and everyday life. Our core strength is computer vision and medical image analysis, supported by explainable machine learning so that experts can understand and trust model decisions.

Alongside imaging, we work on health data analytics, natural language processing for Bangla, cybersecurity for connected systems, and human behavior. We aim to turn data into reliable insights that improve lives.

Explore our publications, learn about our research projects, meet our team, or get in touch.

Research areas

Computer vision & medical image analysis

Deep learning for disease detection from medical images, with explanations clinicians can check. Our work includes an ensemble of Vision Transformer, MobileNet and EfficientNet models for kidney disease diagnosis from 12,000 CT scans, a funded project on multi-class chest disease prediction from X-rays with automated report generation, and a comparison of ConvNeXt and Swin Transformer for osteoporosis classification on imbalanced bone X-rays.

We also apply vision to agriculture and the environment, including tea leaf disease severity estimation and real-time fish disease detection.

Health data analytics

Explainable models for diabetes, obesity, respiratory risk and other health outcomes, built on hospital records and survey data our team collects.

Cybersecurity for connected systems

Intrusion detection for connected vehicles, obfuscated XSS detection, and adversarial robustness of machine learning security detectors.

Natural language processing

Language technology for Bangla, a widely spoken but under-resourced language, including target-aware hate speech classification and misinformation detection with fine-tuned BanglaBERT and LLM-based explanations.

Explainable AI & human behavior

SHAP, LIME and partial dependence analysis to explain predictions, applied to studies of sleep, stress, daily routines and productivity.

Latest updates

  • Paper accepted, COMPAS 2026

    Our paper “BanglaIEHS: A Novel Dataset and Multi-Task Contrastive Framework for Target-Aware Bengali Hate Speech Classification” has been accepted at the IEEE 3rd International Conference on Computing, Applications and Systems (COMPAS 2026), University of Dhaka. It introduces a dataset of 14,003 Bangla social media comments labeled for hate intensity and target group, and a multi-task contrastive model built on BanglaBERT.

  • New project, October 2026

    We have started a new thesis project on cross-layer intrusion detection for connected vehicles, which fuses V2X misbehavior detection with CAN bus intrusion detection and introduces a pivot-detection-latency metric.

  • Publications, 2026

    Twelve ARIFEX Lab papers were published at the 2026 IEEE QPAIN conference and are now available on IEEE Xplore. See our publications.

  • Grant awarded

    Leading University (RPCC & CRISP) has funded our project “A Machine Learning-Based Framework for Multi-Class Chest Disease Prediction and Automated Diagnostic Report Generation”, which aims to help clinicians with automated disease detection and reporting.
