Publications

Peer-reviewed work from the lab. For a full and current list, see Google Scholar.

Published

  • A Novel Ensemble-Based Deep Learning Model for Kidney Disease Diagnosis

    Advances in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, 5(1), 3425–3445, 2025

    A heterogeneous ensemble of Vision Transformer, MobileNet and EfficientNet models on 12,000 CT scans, with LIME explanations for clinical interpretability.

    DOI: 10.54364/AAIML.2025.51196

  • Heart Disease Prediction through Enhanced Machine Learning and Feature Selection

    2024 IEEE 10th International Conference on Smart Instrumentation, Measurement and Applications (ICSIMA), Indonesia

    Correlation analysis and LASSO feature selection with Random Forest reached 97.48% accuracy across harmonized public cohorts.

    DOI: 10.1109/ICSIMA62563.2024.10675564

  • Optimizing Facial Recognition: Traditional vs Deep Learning Approaches

    2024 International Conference on Data Science and Its Applications (ICoDSA), IEEE, Indonesia

    Compared PCA, SVM, CNN and MobileNet transfer learning, showing that classical models can outperform deep models on small samples.

    DOI: 10.1109/ICoDSA62899.2024.10651632

  • Machine Learning Based Respiratory Risk Assessment with Explainable Air Quality Indicators

    QPAIN 2026, IEEE, Bangladesh

    A threshold-optimized ensemble of six classifiers with SHAP explanations, linking environmental exposure to respiratory risk (5,811 observations).

  • Real-Time Image Based Fish Disease Detection with Treatment Recommendation Application

    QPAIN 2026, IEEE, Bangladesh

    An InceptionV3 transfer-learning system for seven freshwater fish conditions, with literature-based treatment recommendations.

Accepted

  • Enhancing Misinformation Detection in Bangla Social Media

    Accepted, COMPAS 2026, IEEE, Bangladesh

    A hybrid framework combining fine-tuned BanglaBERT, stemming, threshold optimization and LLM-based explanations, evaluated on BanFakeNews and real-world social media data.
